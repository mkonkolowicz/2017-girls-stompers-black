Game Calls Playbook

Seven calls Coach yells from the sideline. Each one matches a picture in your head and two drills we do at practice. Hear the call, see the picture, do your job.

Every diagram plays like a short video. Tap a diagram to pause it, ↺ to watch again, ⛶ for full screen. On a phone, turn it sideways to go full screen.

STOMP!SHAPE UP!WINGS OUT!TAP & BLAST!WALL UP!CRASH THE BOX!LOCK THE BOX!

How it works

  1. Learn the picture. Every call has one big picture showing where everyone goes.
  2. Do the drills. At practice, Coach uses the same call during the drill so it sticks.
  3. Hear it in a game? Act fast. No thinking. Call, picture, go.
  4. Repeat it. When Coach yells a call, say it out loud to your teammates too.

Pressing · when the other team has the ball
STOMP!
Go get the ball back, together. One girl presses, one covers, the rest squeeze in.

The picture in your head

  • 1 · STOMPThe closest girl sprints at the ball. Two big steps away, slow down, bend your knees, stand sideways.
  • Make her go to the lineBlock the middle so she has to go to the sideline. The sideline is our extra defender.
  • 2 · COVERThe next closest girl stands behind and to the side of the presser, about 4 big steps away. If she gets past #1, you're there.
  • 3 · SQUEEZEEveryone else moves toward the ball and steps up so the field gets small.

Presser shouts: "I've got ball!"   Cover shouts: "I'm behind you!"

Remember the retreat line: their keeper has the ball, or it's their goal kick? No STOMP yet. Run back behind the retreat line and wait. When one of their players touches the keeper's pass, the ball is in play. Then STOMP!

DRILL 1

1v1 Stomp to the Line

Grid about 20 × 15 big steps · pairs · 8–10 min

  1. Defender (D) starts on the top line with the ball. Attacker (A) waits on the bottom line.
  2. D passes to A, then yells "STOMP!" and sprints at her.
  3. Two big steps away: brakes on. Small steps, knees bent, sideways stance.
  4. D blocks the middle gate and pushes A toward a side Trap Zone.
  5. A scores by dribbling through the middle gate. D scores by winning the ball or trapping A in a side zone.
  6. Swap roles and go to the back of the line.

Remember: sprint, brake, sit. Don't dive in. Watch the ball, not her fancy feet.

Harder: D starts further back, or A gets 2 tries.

DRILL 2

2v2 Press & Cover

Grid about 25 × 20 big steps · 2v2 · 10 min

  1. Two defenders on the top line, two attackers on the bottom line. Two small gates on the top line.
  2. Coach passes to either attacker.
  3. The closest defender yells "STOMP!" and presses.
  4. The other defender yells "COVER!" and moves behind and to the side of her, not flat next to her.
  5. If the attackers pass, switch jobs fast: the new closest girl presses, the other covers.
  6. Attackers score by dribbling through a gate. Defenders score by winning it and dribbling over the bottom line.

Remember: cover makes a diagonal line, not a flat line. Talk the whole time.

Harder: make it 3v2. The defenders have to press and cover even faster.

Positioning · any time
SHAPE UP!
Find your spot in our 2-3-1. Make triangles. Then Coach may add BIG! or SMALL!
  • Our spotsGK · 2 backs (LB, RB) · 3 mids (LM, CM, RM) · 1 striker (S).
  • TrianglesLook around. Can you see two teammates making a triangle with you? That means you always have a pass.
  • Don't bunchIf two Stompers are close enough to high-five, one of you is in the wrong spot.
  • Rubber bandWe're all tied together. When the ball moves, the whole team slides together.
DRILL 1

Shadow Shape (Rubber Band)

Half or full field · whole team · 8 min · no opponents

  1. Everyone stands in their 2-3-1 spot.
  2. Coach (C) carries the ball and walks, then jogs, to a new spot on the field.
  3. The whole team slides together toward the ball side. Nobody stays behind.
  4. Coach yells "BIG!" and everyone spreads wide and long. Coach yells "SMALL!" and everyone squeezes in, between the ball and our goal.
  5. Coach yells "FREEZE!" Check: can you see the ball? Are you in a triangle?

Remember: watch the ball, then look at your neighbours.

Harder: two coaches pass the ball around fast, so the team has to keep sliding.

DRILL 2

Freeze-Frame Scrimmage

Small-sided or 7v7 scrimmage · 12 min

  1. Play a normal scrimmage.
  2. Coach blows the whistle and yells "FREEZE!" Everyone becomes a statue.
  3. Coach picks a girl: "Are you in your spot?" If not, she walks to the right spot.
  4. The girl with the ball points to her two triangle teammates.
  5. Coach yells "SHAPE UP!" Everyone fixes their spot, then play starts again.
  6. 3 perfect freezes = a Stompers point.

Remember: wide players should be close to the sideline when we have the ball.

Harder: Coach only gives 3 seconds to fix it.

Moving the ball forward · we just won it, or our keeper has it
WINGS OUT!
We've got it! Spread out like wings so the girl with the ball has lots of passes. Then move it forward. It's like BIG!, but fast.

The picture in your head

  • WingsLB and RB: run to the sideline, as wide as you can go. LM and RM: wide and up the field.
  • ShowCM: come toward the ball in the middle so it can come to you. Striker: stay high and stretch them.
  • Ball girlHead up. Pass to the open wing, or dribble into the space in front of you. Forward first!
  • GKWait until every red is behind the retreat line. No big boots: roll it, throw it, or pass it on the ground to a wide back.
  • Retreat lineThey can't cross it until one of us touches the keeper's pass. That's free time. Use it!

After STOMP! The girl who wins it yells "WINGS OUT!" Everyone else flies wide and forward right away, before they get back.

DRILL 1

Stomp & Fly

Grid about 30 × 20 big steps · 3 Stompers vs 2 · 10 min

  1. Mark a Wing Lane along each sideline with cones. Three Stompers start near our end line. Two red players start in the middle.
  2. Coach passes to a red player.
  3. The closest Stomper yells "STOMP!" and presses. The next girl covers.
  4. When we win it, that girl yells "WINGS OUT!" The other two sprint into a Wing Lane, wide and forward.
  5. Pass to a wing. She dribbles over the far end line = 1 point. Do it in 6 seconds = 2 points!
  6. Reds score by dribbling over our end line. Swap teams after 5 tries.

Remember: the second we win it, fly wide. Don't stand and watch.

Harder: add a third red player who chases from our end line.

DRILL 2

Retreat Line Build-Out

Our third of the field · GK + 2 backs + CM vs 3 · retreat line cones · 12 min

  1. Put cones on the retreat line and two gates on the halfway line. Reds start near our box.
  2. Coach rolls the ball to our GK. Reds jog back behind the retreat line.
  3. Coach yells "WINGS OUT!" Backs run to the sideline, wide as the cones. CM comes to show in the middle.
  4. GK waits until every red is behind the line, then passes on the ground to a wide back.
  5. She touches it: now reds can cross! Dribble forward or pass to CM.
  6. Through a halfway gate = 2 points. Reds win it and score = 1 point for them.

Remember: no rush. The reds can't come until we touch the pass, so GK takes her time.

Harder: add a fourth red, or start with a goal kick instead of a roll.

Taking an indirect free kick
TAP & BLAST!
Ref's arm is up, so you can't score straight from the kick. Someone else has to touch it first. Tap it, then blast it.

The picture in your head

  • T · TapperStands next to the ball. Rolls it one small step to the side. Just enough so it moves.
  • B · BlasterWaits 3 big steps back. Starts running as the tapper touches it. Shoots first time at a low corner.
  • R · ReboundOne girl starts wide and crashes to the goal when the shot goes, in case the keeper drops it.
  • Backs stay homeOur two backs and GK stay back in case they get the ball.

Why: on an indirect kick, a goal only counts if another player touched the ball first. The tap is that touch.

DRILL 1

Tap & Blast Lines

Edge of the box · two lines + keeper · 10 min

  1. Ball on a cone at the edge of the box. Tappers line up on the left, Blasters on the right.
  2. The Blaster calls "TAP!"
  3. The Tapper rolls the ball one small step sideways. Not too far!
  4. The Blaster runs onto it and shoots first time, low, at a corner.
  5. Tapper joins the Blaster line. Blaster gets her ball and joins the Tapper line.

Remember: soft tap. Laces, toe pointed down, land on your shooting foot.

Harder: put a wall of cones in front, or count to 3: shoot before "3".

DRILL 2

Beat the Wall

3 attackers vs wall of 3 + keeper · 10 min

  1. Coach puts the ball down, raises an arm and says "Indirect!"
  2. Attackers pick jobs: Tapper, Blaster, Rebound. Use a secret hand signal for which side to tap.
  3. The wall can't move until the first touch. Then it charges!
  4. The Blaster has to shoot before the wall gets there. Rebound girl follows the shot in.
  5. Goal = 2 points, rebound goal = 3. Block by the wall = 1 point for the wall.
  6. Rotate so everyone tries every job.

Remember: fast and sneaky. The longer you wait, the closer the wall gets.

Defending an indirect free kick
WALL UP!
Build a wall that blocks the near post. Keeper sees the ball. When they touch it, we CHARGE.

The picture in your head

  • GK · BossShouts "WALL, 3!" and lines them up. Stands where she can see the ball.
  • W · WallThe Wall Captain stands on the line from the ball to the near post. Two teammates squeeze in next to her, toward the middle. Shoulder to shoulder, arms crossed over your chest, eyes on the ball.
  • CH · ChargerOur fastest girl stands just off the wall. On the first touch she sprints at the ball to block the shot.
  • M · MarkerStays goal-side of any open attacker.
  • S · OutletStriker stays up field, ready for the clearance.

Distance: back up until the referee says stop. Don't move until they kick it.

DRILL 1

Wall-Up Race

Around our box · whole team · 8 min · use a stopwatch

  1. Mark 3 ball spots around the box (1, 2, 3). Team jogs around the middle of the field.
  2. Coach puts the ball on a spot and yells "WALL UP!"
  3. GK shouts "WALL, 3!" and names the three girls.
  4. The Wall Captain finds the near-post line. Two more squeeze in next to her toward the middle.
  5. Arms crossed, feet together, eyes up. Charger and Marker find their spots.
  6. Coach times it. Beat the team record!

Remember: the keeper must still see the ball. Tallest girl on the outside.

DRILL 2

Charge the Tap

Wall of 3 + Charger + GK vs Tapper & Shooter · 10 min

  1. Wall, Charger and GK set up on "WALL UP!"
  2. Attackers do their tap-and-shoot.
  3. On the first touch, the Charger sprints out and blocks the shot with her body (not her hands!).
  4. The wall holds still for the shot, then everyone attacks the rebound.
  5. Block or save = 1 point for the defenders. Goal = 1 point for the attackers.
  6. Rotate Charger and Wall Captain.

Remember: charge on the touch, not before. Too early and the ref makes us do it again.

Taking a corner kick
CRASH THE BOX!
Kicker puts her hand up: get ready. Her hand comes down: GO! Runners attack three spots: near post, far post and the rebound.

The picture in your head

  • K · KickerWide mid on that side. Hand UP = get ready. Hand DOWN = GO! Aim for The Spot, the space in front of the goal.
  • Near postStriker starts outside the box and runs hard to the front post.
  • Far postOther wide mid runs to the back post for tap-ins.
  • ReboundCM waits at the top of the box for anything that comes out. Shoot it!
  • Stay homeBoth backs and the GK stay back to stop a fast break.

Start outside, then run in. Runners who are already moving are hard to mark.

DRILL 1

Hit the Spot

Corner flag · kickers line · 3 target zones · 8 min

  1. Kickers line up at the corner flag, each with a ball.
  2. Put the ball in the corner arc. Take 3–4 steps back and a little to the side.
  3. Plant foot next to the ball. Strike under the middle of the ball with your laces or the inside of your foot.
  4. Land it in a zone: Near or Far = 1 point, The Spot = 2 points.
  5. Switch to the other corner so both feet get practice.

Remember: come in at an angle, lean back a little, follow through toward the target.

U10 tip: a hard, low ball to the near post works great too!

DRILL 2

Crash the Box

Kicker + 3 runners vs 2 defenders + keeper · 10 min

  1. Runners start outside the top of the box.
  2. Kicker puts her hand up. Runners get ready outside the box.
  3. Kicker drops her hand: that's GO! Everyone yells "CRASH!" and sprints to their spots: near post, far post, rebound.
  4. Try to arrive at the same time as the ball. Near post flicks it on, far post taps it in, rebound girl shoots.
  5. 5 corners, then rotate jobs. Goal = 1 point, rebound goal = 2.

Remember: don't stand and wait in the box. Start outside, then run in.

Harder: add a third defender.

Defending a corner kick
LOCK THE BOX!
Everyone has a job: Post, Markers, Gate, Outlet. Clear it HIGH, FAR, WIDE, then everybody steps OUT.

The picture in your head

  • GKMiddle of the goal, a little toward the back post. Shouts "KEEPER!" if she's catching it.
  • P · PostStands right next to the near post and kicks away anything that comes there.
  • M · MarkersEach one picks an attacker and stands goal-side, between her and our goal. Keep one hand lightly on her and your eyes on the ball.
  • G · GateStands out toward the corner, the distance the ref sets, to block a short corner.
  • S · OutletStriker waits up the field for the clear. Go!

Clearing: HIGH, FAR, WIDE, out toward the sideline. Never kick it across the front of our goal. After the clear, everyone yells "OUT!" and steps up together.

DRILL 1

Mark & Clear

Pairs in the box · Coach serves from the corner · 10 min

  1. Pair up: one attacker, one defender. The defender stands goal-side with one hand lightly on the attacker. No grabbing!
  2. Coach serves from the corner. Throw it at first, then kick it.
  3. Defender attacks the ball first, yells "MINE!" and clears it high and wide into a Clear Zone.
  4. Into a Clear Zone = 2 points, any clear = 1. Attacker scores = 1 point for the attacker.
  5. Swap roles.

Remember: watch the ball and your girl. Kick through the bottom of the ball to get it high.

DRILL 2

Lock the Box Game

Kicker + 3 attackers vs full corner defence · gates on halfway · 12 min

  1. Coach yells "LOCK THE BOX!" Defenders find their jobs: GK, Post, 3 Markers, Gate, Outlet.
  2. Attackers take the corner and try to score.
  3. Once it's cleared, every defender yells "OUT!" and sprints up to the top of the box together.
  4. The Outlet gets the clear and dribbles through a halfway gate = 2 points. Any clear out of the box = 1 point. Attacker goal = 2 points for the attackers.
  5. 5 corners, then rotate so everyone tries Post, Marker, Gate and Outlet.

Remember: stepping OUT together pushes their players away from our goal.
