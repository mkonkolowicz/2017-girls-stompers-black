# Changelog

Every change to the LaSalle Stompers 2017 Girls Black game calls playbook.
Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): new calls or drills bump the minor number, and fixes bump the patch number.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-12
The playbook now uses the coaches' sideline words exactly, so players, coaches and parents all say the same thing.

### Changed
- **SHAPE UP!** is now **SHAPE!**, and **BIG!** is now **SHAPE!** too (spread out to your diamond spot). GET SMALL! stays.
- **STOMP!** is now **PRESS!** Drills renamed: "1v1 Press to the Line" and "Press & Fly".
- Sideline Words cards now say where each word is in the playbook ("In the playbook: ...").
- Feedback form choices renamed to SHAPE! and PRESS!

### Removed
- The "Short version" boxes in SHAPE! and PRESS!: the call and the sideline word are now the same.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-12
Our formation is now the head coach's **diamond**.

### Changed
- SHAPE UP!: the team lines up in two diamonds. The back diamond is GK, LB, RB and CM. The front diamond is CM, LM, RM and S. CM sits just in front of the backs and is in both diamonds. LM and RM play higher and wider.
- The BIG! and GET SMALL! pictures now show the diamonds stretching and shrinking. Shadow Shape (Drill 1) starts in the diamond.
- New "Two diamonds" tip in SHAPE UP!
- WINGS OUT!: the starting spots in the picture match the diamond.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-12
Lines the playbook up with the coaching staff's U-10 Offseason Development Plan.

### Added
- **Sideline Words**: Shape! Get Small! Time! Take Space! Press! Mark Up! What each one means, when you'll hear it, and which call it matches. Includes the tip for parents: say "Time!" or "Take Space!" instead of "Kick it!" or "Pass it!"
- **At Home**: five ways to practise in 10 to 15 minutes a day: Daily Touch, Wall Ball, Target Practice, Watch & Talk Soccer, Free Play & 1v1s.
- SHAPE UP!: "Every spot" (everyone learns every position) and "SHAPE!" as the short version.
- STOMP!: "Lost it? Sprint back" and "PRESS!" as the short word.
- WINGS OUT!: "Want the ball!" Move into space and call for it.
- LOCK THE BOX!: Markers hear "MARK UP!", and a "Back post" job: watch for a girl sneaking there.
- WALL UP!: the Charger is first a **Stopper**, standing in front of the ball to stop a quick kick. New "Close or far?" rule: close = wall, far = everyone MARK UP!

### Changed
- "SMALL!" is now **"GET SMALL!"**, to match the coaches' words.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-11
### Changed
- Round the Gate moved from SHAPE UP! to **WINGS OUT!** (now WINGS OUT! Drill 3). It's a drill for advancing the ball, not for team shape. Added an "In a game" tip that links it to WINGS OUT!

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-10
### Added
- **SHAPE UP! Drill 3: Round the Gate (Third-Girl Run).** The ball goes through a gate of two tall cones, and the runner goes round it. L starts her run on G's pass, not on the through ball. Added at the head coach's request, adapted from a Teloframe drill video.

First versioned release, made just for the LaSalle Stompers 2017 Girls Black team. This is the version we're sharing to ask for feedback.

### Changed

- Title is now "LaSalle Stompers 2017 Girls Black" everywhere: page, app name and PDF.
- The PDF is now called `2017-girls-stompers-black-playbook.pdf`.

- Feedback form. Every call has a "💬 Feedback on this call" button that opens a short Google Form with that call already picked. The footer has a "Send feedback" link for the whole playbook. The buttons are hidden when printing.

- `tools/create-feedback-form.gs`: the Apps Script that built the form.
- This changelog.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-09
### Added
- **OVER THE TOP!**: when the press covers our wings, the keeper throws or kicks it long behind them. Includes the Throw & Chase and Win the Second Ball drills.
- Calls grouped by situation: Team shape, Win the ball, Advance the ball, Corners, Free kicks.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-09
### Added
- **SET THE TRAP!**: their keeper has the ball. We line up at the retreat line, then the striker presses, the wide mids block, CM jams the middle, and the backs push up but not too far. Includes the Line Up & Pounce and Over the Trap drills.
### Changed
- The STOMP! retreat-line reminder now points to SET THE TRAP!
